# Global Olympic Sports Performance Analysis

## Project Overview
An interactive Tableau dashboard and data analysis project examining historical Olympic data from 1992 to 2026. The study focuses on analyzing medal achievements across nations, athlete participation trends, regional performance efficiency, and sport category-wise medal distributions to drive data-backed decisions for sports organizations and policymakers.

## Project Scenarios & Use Cases

### Scenario 1: Ms. Priya — National Olympic Committee Performance Strategist
- **Objective:** Identify which sports categories and disciplines to invest in ahead of the upcoming Olympic cycle using 8 years of historical medal data across 60 countries and 50 sports.
- **Key Insights:** Utilized average medals per athlete by sport category (e.g., Rowing, Shooting) to determine high-yield categories and compare national performance against top performers like the United States and China.

### Scenario 2: Mr. Carlos — Sports Investment Analyst at an International Funding Agency
- **Objective:** Evaluate the relationship between national sports investments and actual medal performance over multiple Olympic cycles.
- **Key Insights:** Analyzed investment efficiency bubbles and regional efficiency percentages (e.g., South America and Europe/Asia) to guide grant distributions and support underfunded, high-potential regions.

### Scenario 3: William Robert — Broadcast Journalist and Data Storyteller
- **Objective:** Prepare compelling, data-backed narratives for a feature documentary on the evolution of Olympic competition from 1992 to 2026.
- **Key Insights:** Highlighted trends in total medals awarded across host nations (such as Japan, US, and China) and visualized regional medal distributions.

## Technical Architecture & Tools
- **Tools Used:** Tableau Public, GitHub, Data Preprocessing, Data Visualization
- **Data Scope:** Historical Olympic data (1992–2026) across multiple countries and sports disciplines.
